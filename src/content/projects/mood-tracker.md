TLDR: Exhaustive-depressive-all-of-the-above day suck up your energy. This app designed to help you keep track your mood swings easily, effortlessly, energy-less-ly.

A Telegram bot for daily mood journaling that stores entries and assisted by AI for reflective prompts. Built end-to-end: bot UX, API, persistence, and model integration. A small system that has to stay reliable because people use it when they are not at a desk.
