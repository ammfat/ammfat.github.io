BLIV is the analytical backbone for Indonesia's Ministry of Home Affairs. As Technical Lead I own direction across delivery, reliability, and how stakeholder needs become engineering work.

The constraint is public-service uptime: when the warehouse or BI layer fails, reporting for government operations fails with it. The work is less about greenfield features and more about keeping a living platform honest — migrations that don't strand consumers, monitoring that catches drift before users do, and a team that can ship without waiting on one person.
