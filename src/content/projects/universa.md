At Universa AI I co-authored the foundational agent base classes, the tool layer, and graph-based orchestration on a model-agnostic Graph-of-Thought and MCP-like framework.

The goal was structured reasoning from non-reasoning LLMs: agents that produce functional software artifacts instead of vibes. Alongside that, I ran three intern squads (15+) shipping NLP classifiers, RAG pipelines, fine-tuning, and synthetic data orchestration.
